OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

LexiStitch & LexiStitch Pro

Effective Date: November 3, 2026  |  Developer: Purnendu Studios  |  Contact: purnendustudios.support@gmail.com

Purnendu Studios ("we", "our", or "us") built the LexiStitch (and LexiStitch Pro) Android application (package ID: com.pkstudios.lexistitch). This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles permissions, processes images and text locally on your device, and interacts with third-party services.

🔒 Core 100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee:
All screen captures, OpenCV image stitching, canvas annotations, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) text extraction happen 100% locally on your Android device. We do not operate external cloud servers that store, view, or collect your screenshots or extracted text.

1. Permissions & Sensitive APIs We Use (And Why)

  • AccessibilityService API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE — AutoScrollService): LexiStitch Pro optionally uses Android's AccessibilityService API solely to perform automated vertical screen-scrolling gestures when you explicitly tap the "Auto" capture button, and to detect hardware Volume Down key presses for emergency capture stops.
    Strict Guarantee: We never use AccessibilityService to read, monitor, collect, store, or transmit your screen content, keystrokes, passwords, or personal data.
  • Screen Capture / MediaProjection API (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION): Used only when you actively initiate a screen capture session and grant single-session system consent. Captured frames are stitched in local device memory and saved exclusively to your phone's local storage.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to display the floating capture controls (Manual, Auto, Pause, Stop) and live preview pod over the app you are capturing.
  • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Used optionally during active screen recording to temporarily silence incoming notification banners so they do not cover your stitched screenshot. Your previous notification filter is automatically restored the moment capture pauses or ends.

2. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) & Google Lens

  • In-App Offline OCR (Google ML Kit & Tesseract): All text recognition inside LexiStitch runs locally on your device. On-demand offline neural language packs are downloaded directly from Google Play Services or the official Tesseract GitHub repository (`jsdelivr` / `github.com`) and stored locally on your device.
  • Google Lens Integration: If you explicitly tap the "Lens" button to send a screenshot or slice to the external Google Lens app (`com.google.ar.lens`), that image is shared with the Google Lens app on your device and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party SDKs (Analytics, Crash Reporting, Ads & Billing)

While your screenshots and extracted text never leave your device, the app integrates standard Google SDKs for stability, monetization, and licensing:

  • Google AdMob (Free Tier Only): In the Free tier, Google AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID, coarse location (country/region), and ad interaction diagnostics to serve banners and interstitial ads. Upgrading to LexiStitch Pro permanently disables all AdMob ad requests.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash stack traces, device model, and Android OS version so we can diagnose and fix application crashes. No screenshots or OCR text are ever included in crash logs.
  • Google Play Billing Library: All Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases (`pro_monthly`, `pro_yearly`, `pro_lifetime`, `pro_lifetime_flash`) are processed securely by Google Play. We do not access or store your credit card, UPI, or bank details.

4. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Data

  • All captured screenshots are stored locally on your device inside the app's private capture directory (or exported to your system Gallery/Files only when you tap Save).
  • You can permanently delete individual screenshots or tap [ 🗑️ DELETE ALL ] inside the Navigation Drawer at any time, or enable the 7-day / 30-day Auto-Clean rule. Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all internal app storage.

5. Children's Privacy

LexiStitch does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
